Paired lamps

Pair a lamp

Add another lamp to this group

Start pairing here, then hold the group button on the other lamp for 3 seconds until it pulses. Both lamps blink twice when they find each other. Or hold the group button on both lamps, without this app.

The group button

One tap turns every paired lamp on. Keep tapping to step through every combination: each lamp alone, then pairs, and so on, ending with all off. After a 5 second pause the next tap starts over.

Brightness steps

Two taps on the lamp button step through these. 1 to 100%, up to 6 steps.

Warm / cool steps

Three taps step through these. 0 is the warmest, 100 the coolest, up to 6 steps.

Brightness limit

Limits every step. The lowest step stays as it is and the steps above it are squeezed in under the limit. Lower means longer battery life.
