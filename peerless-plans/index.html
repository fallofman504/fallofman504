How the AI and QA work

Fast because AI agents draft it. Trusted because a person signs it.

We say it plainly: trained AI agents do the drafting. That is why the price is flat and the turnaround is a day. Every job then passes the same six gates.

  1. IntakeWe confirm the files open, the units are known, and the scale checks against a stated dimension.
  2. NormalizeLayers cleaned, units set, every suite outline closed, saved to the building's base file.
  3. ProduceThe producing agent calculates the areas, draws the test fit, or drafts the exhibit.
  4. Independent QAA separate agent that did not produce the work runs the full checklist.
  5. Human sign-offA named person reviews the sheet and the QA report, then initials the title block.
  6. Deliver and archiveYou receive the PDF, the DWG, and a one-page QA sheet.

The QA sheet

Every deliverable ships with one page listing the source files used, the measurement method, the checks run, and what was not verified.

Your files stay yours

Client drawings, rent rolls, and leases stay confidential, are not used to train models, and are deleted on request.

What we are not

Test fits are conceptual space plans, not for construction or permit. Areas are calculated from the drawings you provide unless a field check is ordered.
